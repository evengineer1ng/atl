Broker — Shared Market Tape

The broker is ATL's shared market tape: one on-disk OHLCV feed that every lobby slot reads, so experiments run container-free in pure pandas instead of one Freqtrade bot each. This is the cheap broad-exploration layer. Ghost evidence is scouting-grade — directional, calibrated against Freqtrade, not a substitute for it. Freqtrade stays the scarce validation layer.

Quarterly Champion

Every three completed major-league seasons, ATL files a formal report ranking the official teams and naming a Quarterly Champion. Think of it as a parole hearing, not a leaderboard: winning the quarter does not deploy real money — it makes the strongest organism eligible for commissioner review. This is the disciplined bridge toward a future real-money Capital League, designed so deployment is rare, structured, archived, and evidence-based — never impulsive.

What it measures

Peer-relative percentile scoring across the official season metrics ATL already tracks — no new "can this make money?" test. The whole league is the test.

Recency weighting

The three seasons are weighted {{ (recency_weights[0]*100)|int }}% / {{ (recency_weights[1]*100)|int }}% / {{ (recency_weights[2]*100)|int }}% — lately matters most, but a full quarter of evidence is still required.

No live race

There is intentionally no projected standing and no current leader. A ranking appears only once a quarter has fully closed, so nobody optimizes emotionally around an unfinished quarter.
